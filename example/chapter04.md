# The Waystone's Warnings

The waystone at the fork had more to say than most. Someone had carved a
proverb into its face long before Spell was born:

``` cite
A word restored is worth more than ten invented.
(Unknown, and likely proud of it)
```

Beneath it, fresher hands had added their own notes.

``` note
The northern path floods every spring.
Bring dry boots, or don't complain about wet ones.
```

``` info
A well fed further along, past the second bend.
```

``` warn
Something took up residence in the old mill --- give it the road, not an
argument.
```

Spell read all four twice, the way he read anything carved in stone, and
chose the southern path.
