# Packing the Satchel

Before the road, there was the sorting. Spell's satchel held whatever had
survived the last three ruins, and none of it sorted itself:

- A stub of chalk, for marking safe walls
- A folded map, more folds than paper by now
  - Torn at the northern edge
  - Annotated in someone else's hand
    - In red ink, unhelpfully
- Ash's spare feather, for luck

And once the satchel was packed, the same short ritual as always, in the
same short order:

1. Close the satchel
2. Check the buckle twice
   1. Once by hand
   2. Once by ear, for the click
3. Say the old words, quietly

He said them. The road answered the way roads do -- by simply being there,
waiting.
