# Threads Through the Grimoire

Some passages need to be found again later, so Spell learned to mark them
properly rather than trust his memory.

{#waystone-choice}
This is the fork where he chose the southern path over the flooded one. If
the story doubles back --- and stories about crossroads usually do --- this
is where it doubles back to.

A page can call back to itself: [return to the fork](#waystone-choice).

It can also call forward to a page that hasn't been written yet, and trust
that it will be, by the time anyone reads this:
[skip ahead to where the road ends](#road-ends).
