# A Page from Another Book

The old archive had collapsed long before Spell reached it --- roof gone,
shelves gone, most of the books gone with them. But not all of them. Wedged
beneath a fallen beam, still dry, still whole, he found a single chronicle
that had somehow survived everything the building hadn't.

Restoring a page like this means one thing above all: changing nothing.
Spell copied it whole, exactly as it was written, and bound it into this
very book without altering a single word.

A find like that deserves its own page.
