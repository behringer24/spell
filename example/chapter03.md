# Letters Along the Road

Word travels slowly between wandering restorers, but it travels. Spell kept
in touch with the only two people who might still remember his name: the
keepers of [the Archive of Wandering Scribes](https://github.com/behringer24/spell),
where every restored page eventually finds a shelf, and whoever still tends
[its collected notes](https://github.com/behringer24/spell/wiki "for those who like their lore properly indexed").

Neither had written back in some time. That, too, was normal.
