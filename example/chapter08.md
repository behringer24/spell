# The Grand Index

The Archive kept no shelves in order until someone indexed them, so Spell
did what restorers always end up doing eventually: he catalogued.

The %[binding press](Inventions) came first in his notes, same as it always
does; the %[bound-leaf technique](Inventions) beside it, since one rarely
appears in old chronicles without the other.

Further in, %[the everlight lantern](Inventions|Everlight lantern) took an
entire shelf of its own. By the time %[everlight lanterns](Inventions|Everlight lantern)
were lighting %[everlight-bound devices](Inventions|Everlight lantern)
across three centuries of chronicles, Spell had stopped trying to fit them
under one heading and simply let the index sort it out --- which is, after
all, the entire point of keeping one.

Names filled a second list. %[Maer Thallow](Persons) belonged there, and so
did %[Iva Sennett](Persons), who Spell noted --- with the particular
satisfaction of a cataloguer catching a good cross-reference --- had
applied %[everlight power](Inventions|Everlight lantern) to the waystone
network a full generation after Thallow first tamed it for a lantern.
